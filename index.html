NIGERIA CURRENCY

The Official Currency of Nigeria

Nigeria's currency is one of the most important financial symbols in the country. The Naira is the official monetary unit of Nigeria.

About The Naira

It is used for buying and selling goods and services, paying salaries, saving money, paying taxes, conducting business and settling debts.

  1. ₦1 = 100 kobo
  2. ₦0.50 = 50 kobo
  3. ₦500 = 50,000 kobo
  4. ₦1000 = 100,000 kobo

History of Nigerian Currency

Colonial Period: The West African Currency Board (WACB) issued currency for Nigeria and other British West African territories.

1959: First Nigerian currency issued. On 1 July 1959, the Central Bank of Nigeria issued Nigerian banknotes and coins:

  • £5, £1, 10 shillings, 5 shillings

1962: The wording was changed to reflect Nigeria's republican status, from "FEDERATION OF NIGERIA" to "FEDERAL REPUBLIC OF NIGERIA".

1973: Nigeria abandoned the pound, shilling and pence system and introduced Naira and Kobo:

  1. ₦10, ₦5, ₦1, 50 kobo
